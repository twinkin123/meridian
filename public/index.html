03 / Flow

Three steps, no paperwork

You get a dedicated deposit address the moment you sign up. Everything after that is one button.

01

Fund your address

Send at least $10 of USDC to your personal deposit address. It is swept into treasury and credited once confirmed.

02

Earn daily

03

Withdraw on demand

Request any amount above $10 to your own wallet. Large requests need a second approval, then pay out on-chain.

05 / Questions

Questions

What is the minimum?

$10 to deposit, $10 to withdraw. Deposits below $10 are held and combined with your next one.

Is there a lock-up?

No. Withdrawals process on demand, subject to a per-address cap that scales with your balance.

Do you need my ID?

No KYC at this stage. Access is geo-restricted, and the platform is operated without a legal entity.

How is the rate set?

A daily credit between 1% and 3.5% of principal, set each day. A 15% performance fee applies above a high-water mark.

Open an account in under a minute

An email and a password. Your deposit address is generated before you finish reading this sentence.
