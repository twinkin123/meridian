06 / Questions

Questions

What is the minimum?

$10 to deposit, $10 to withdraw. A deposit lands in Available; staking it locks for 30 days.

Is there a lock-up?

Only once you stake. A deposit sits in Available; a stake locks for 30 days, then you request a withdrawal.

Do you need my ID?

No KYC at this stage. Access is geo-restricted, and the platform is operated without a legal entity.

What happens at day 30?

The lock releases. Principal and credit move to Available, where you can withdraw it or stake it again for another 30 days.
