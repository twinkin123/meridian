Concept

What Quvio is

Quvio is a custodial yield account. You deposit a supported currency, it is converted and staked as principal, and the live trading book pays a fixed daily credit against that principal. You do not manage positions, claim rewards or roll anything over.

The account is custodial: Quvio holds the keys to the staked balance so the book can be reconciled continuously. Your deposit address is generated the moment you sign up, before you have finished reading this sentence.

Minimum deposit is $10. Principal is locked for 30 days. Credit runs from 1% to 4% daily depending on the size and currency of the position.

Bookkeeping

The ledger

Every movement is written to a double-entry ledger. Each credit is a pair of postings, each withdrawal is a pair, each referral payment is a pair. Nothing is a single-sided write, so the books balance by construction rather than by trust.

The ledger is append-only. A correction is a new posting, never an edit, so any balance can be walked back to the deposit that created it.

Staking

The 30-day lock

Principal locks for 30 days from the deposit. The lock is what lets the book hold a position long enough to pay a fixed rate instead of a floating one.

Credit accrues during the lock and is withdrawable immediately, because it is not principal. At day 30 the principal unlocks and can be withdrawn in full or left in place to keep earning.

Returns

Daily credit

A fixed credit posts to your balance every 24 hours, based on locked principal. No claiming, no compounding tricks, no gas per payout.

The rate is fixed at deposit time and does not move with the market during the lock. Larger positions and certain currencies earn at the higher end of the 1–4% band.

Exits

Withdrawals

Request any amount of your available balance to your own wallet. Approved automatically. Large requests take a second approval, then pay out on-chain.

  1. Open the withdraw panel and enter an amount up to your available balance.
  2. Confirm the destination address. It must be a Solana address you control.
  3. Payout settles in USDC or SOL. Small requests land in seconds.

Locked principal is not part of available balance until day 30. Credit and unlocked principal are.

Trading

Perps

Perpetual futures sit beside the credit account. A position is collateralised from available balance and its result posts to the same ledger, so a win and a loss both show up in the same place as a deposit or a credit.

Perps are optional. A staked position with no open trade still earns the daily credit.

Assets

Swap

Swap moves you between supported currencies at a quoted rate before you deposit, or after a withdrawal lands. Quotes are live and the route is chosen for you.

If you hold something outside the supported list, swap into USDC first and deposit that.

Controls

Security

Deposit addresses are swept by a rent-guarded process, so inbound funds move to treasury automatically rather than accumulating in addresses that cost rent. Three-leg reconciliation compares the journal, treasury balances and chain state; a break stops payouts until it is explained.

  • Append-only ledger with derived balances
  • Rent-guarded sweeper on every inbound address
  • Three-leg recon before payout batches
  • Second approval on large withdrawals
Risk

Risk

The daily credit is fixed per position, but it is not a bank guarantee. The book can have a losing stretch, and a fixed rate is paid from a book that is sometimes down.

  • Market risk on the underlying book while principal is locked
  • Lock risk: principal is not withdrawable for 30 days
  • Custody risk: Quvio holds the staked keys
  • Currency risk between deposit and payout asset

Deposit only what you can leave for a month. The full statement lives in the Risk Disclosures.

FAQ

Short answers

Do I need KYC?

No. An email and a password is the whole signup.

What is the minimum?

$10 equivalent.

When can I withdraw?

Credit any time, principal after day 30.

Is the rate fixed?

The rate on your position is fixed at deposit and does not move during the lock. A new deposit can price differently.

Which network?

Solana, in USDC or SOL. Deposits sent on other networks are not recoverable.

Do referrals pay more than once?

No. One payment per person, on their first deposit.
