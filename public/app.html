How the stake works

1. Deposit and lock

Add USDC or SOL, enter any amount from $10, and lock it for 30 days.

2. Earn daily credit

Your stake earns 1–4% a day. Credit lands in four 6-hour slices, so it can be withdrawn as it arrives.

3. Unlock at day 30

When the term ends the stake matures. Request an unlock and the principal returns to Available after review.

Credit vests in four slices a day

Each day’s credit releases in four 6-hour slices and can be withdrawn as it lands. The 15% fee comes off profit only, never your principal, and the principal itself is untouched until day 30.
